How AgentTrust Works

Three steps from code to verifiable trust. No account required for the free scan.

STEP 1

Point at your code

Run agenttrust scan on a local directory, GitHub repo, or npm package. We resolve everything.

STEP 2

Get your Trust Card

Receive a letter grade (A–F), OWASP-mapped findings, permission manifest, dependency audit, and SARIF output.

STEP 3

Share your badge

Embed your grade badge in your README. Buyers, auditors, and AI hosts see your verified trust score at a glance.

Frequently Asked Questions

      


      


        

          
          

            AgentTrust is a trust and security verification platform for autonomous AI agents and MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers. It scans your codebase for vulnerabilities, maps findings to OWASP Agentic AI Top 10 and OWASP LLM Top 10 2025, generates a verifiable Trust Card with a letter grade, and provides a shareable badge for your README.
          

        


        

          
          

            The audit is a three-step process: 1) We scan your agent or MCP server codebase using static analysis, OWASP rule matching, permission extraction, and dependency auditing. 2) We generate a Trust Card with a weighted security score (0–100) and letter grade (A–F). 3) You receive a detailed report with findings mapped to OWASP codes, remediation guidance, and a shareable badge.
          

        


        

          
          

            A Trust Card is a standardized, verifiable credential that summarizes the security posture of an AI agent or MCP server. It includes a letter grade (A–F), security score (0–100), permission manifest, OWASP-mapped findings, dependency status, and provenance information. It follows the agenttrust/trust-card/v1 schema.
          

        


        

          
          

            Yes. AgentTrust maps findings to both the OWASP Agentic AI Top 10 (ASI01–ASI10, published December 2025) and the OWASP LLM Top 10 2025 (LLM01–LLM10). This includes goal hijacking, tool exploitation, excessive permissions, prompt injection, secrets management, and more.
          

        


        

          
          

            AgentTrust exports permission manifests, human-in-the-loop records, and audit trails that you can attach to an EU AI Act compliance review (e.g. Article 50 transparency records). It is a static scanner, not a certification body — treat the Trust Card as evidence input, not a compliance verdict.
